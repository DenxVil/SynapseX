## About Synapse - MAMC

### Main Information

**Synapse** is the annual cultural and medical festival of **Maulana Azad Medical College (MAMC)**, New Delhi. It is one of the most prestigious medical college festivals in India, attracting thousands of participants from medical institutions across the country.

### Festival Details

**Dates:** March 14-16, 2026 (Example dates)
**Venue:** MAMC Campus, New Delhi
**Theme:** "Healing Through Harmony"
**Expected Participation:** 5000+ students from 50+ colleges

### About MAMC

**Full Name:** Maulana Azad Medical College
**Established:** 1958
**Location:** 2, Bahadur Shah Zafar Marg, New Delhi - 110002
**Type:** Government Medical College
**Affiliation:** University of Delhi
**Recognition:** Medical Council of India (MCI)

**Courses Offered:**
- MBBS (250 seats)
- MD/MS (various specialties)
- DM/MCh (super-specialty courses)
- Diploma courses

**Attached Hospitals:**
- Lok Nayak Hospital (LNJP)
- GB Pant Hospital

**Notable Features:**
- One of the premier medical institutions in India
- Excellent faculty and infrastructure
- Strong research culture
- Active student community
